Cookie Policy

schedule Last Updated: June 24, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how LCM Ministerial College uses cookies and similar storage technologies to secure, support, and enhance your digital experience on our platform.

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored locally on your device (computer, phone, or tablet) by your browser when you visit a website. They help the site identify your session, preserve your preferences, and maintain continuous authentication states.

2. Cookies We Use

We only use cookies that are essential for the performance of our services. These include:

  • Authentication & Security (JWT): We store security tokens (JSON Web Tokens) inside your local storage or cookies. This keeps you safely signed in as you navigate between lessons, grades, and your profile dashboard.
  • Session Identifiers: Secure session cookies used by the PHP backend to coordinate API requests and check permissions.
  • User Preferences: Basic settings (such as dark mode preferences or navigation layouts) stored to maintain consistency.

3. Managing Cookies

Most browsers accept cookies automatically, but you can configure your browser settings to reject or delete cookies. Please note that disabling essential cookies will prevent you from signing in or tracking your lesson progress inside the Student Portal.
