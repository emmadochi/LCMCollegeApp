Privacy Policy

schedule Last Updated: June 24, 2026

At Lifechangers Ministerial College, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of our student community, administrative staff, and website visitors. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when using our platform.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a high-quality learning experience, we collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Credentials: Your full name, email address, and password details registered directly on our student portal.
  • Google Authentication: For users logging in via Google Services, we securely fetch your email, name, and profile image using standard OAuth protocols. We do not store Google account credentials.
  • Academic Progress: Active courses, lesson completions, assignment submissions, quiz results, and certification status to compile your records.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your collected information is strictly utilized to:

  • Establish and secure your student account.
  • Deliver structured lessons, assessments, and grade evaluations.
  • Send email notifications, verification codes, login updates, and graduation announcements.
  • Monitor and prevent abusive activity, spam, and brute-force access attempts.

3. Data Security & Storage

We host our services and database within highly secure, self-hosted environments. Secure password hashing algorithms (BCRYPT) and standard JSON Web Tokens (JWT) are enforced to guard session management and private records. We do NOT share, rent, or sell your personal information to third-party advertisers.

4. Your Rights

Students and administrators have the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of their personal information. Please connect with our support desk using the Contact page to initiate account data adjustments.
